Pronunciation & Audio

Controls the spoken audio used across Pinyin, Dictate, Converse and more.

Speech rate
How fast text-to-speech reads Chinese aloud.
🐢 🐇
Force Stop Audio On Next Card
Cuts off any still-playing audio the moment the next card is shown, in Type Pinyin, Match Pinyin, and Writing Practice.
Audio Delay After Input
How long to wait, in ms, before speaking — after a correct answer in Writing/Typing/Match Pinyin, and after tapping a word's audio button in Search or Word Bank.
Type Pinyin Audio
Speak the word once its pinyin is typed correctly.
Match Pinyin Audio
Speak the word once its initial, final, and tone are matched correctly.
Writing Pinyin Audio
Speak the word once it's drawn correctly. Doesn't apply to Strokes or Radicals practice.

Hanzi Writing Practice

Tunes how the stroke-order quiz in Writing Practice grades you.

Show hint after misses
Number of failed strokes before the correct stroke is shown.
Stroke grading leniency
Higher is more forgiving of imprecise strokes. Default 1.
Stroke fade duration (ms)
How long a mistaken stroke lingers before fading away. Default 400.
Brush stroke width
Multiplier on the thickness of the line drawn as you write. Default 1.0.
Auto-advance multi-character words
Move straight to a word's next character on success, without clicking Next. Only between characters of the same word — moving on to a new word still needs Next.
